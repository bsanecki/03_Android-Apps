## Pandago

A quiz and rewards app. Players duel each other live, play with friends, take daily quizzes, climb global rankings and earn coins that can be exchanged for rewards, including gift cards.

- 1 vs 1 live duels and play with friends
- Daily quiz with a bonus
- World and friends rankings based on trophies
- Coins, Lucky Wheel, VIP Shop and tasks

[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pandago.app) | [Documentation](Pandago.md)

## LocalLock

An app protection and privacy tool. It locks selected apps behind a PIN, password, pattern or biometric authentication, and photographs anyone who fails to unlock them.

- App locking with multiple authentication methods
- Intruder photos from front and back cameras
- Session unlock, anti-uninstall protection and recovery key
- Full authentication history

[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.locallock.app) | [Documentation](LocalLock.md)

## Orbit

A private productivity and life-management app built exclusively for personal use. It combines daily planning, tasks, work orders, finances, investments, habits, goals, notes, ideas, books and quotes in one place.

- Daily dashboard with today's plan
- Calendar, tasks and order tracking
- Finance, investment, habit and goal modules

This is a private project and is not publicly available. [Documentation](Orbit.md)

## CalculatorOS

An experimental app currently in development. It explores a hidden app environment integrated into a calculator-style interface. Details will be added as the project matures.
